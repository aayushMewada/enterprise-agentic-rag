SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY - NOT A REAL SERVICE-LEVEL AGREEMENT

Ticket and Service-Level Policy
Policy ID: SYN-SLA-004
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Ticket categories

Documentation Follow-up is used for missing, stale, expired, or unsigned standard documents.

Compliance Escalation is used for material identity mismatches, suspected fraud indicators, conflicting declarations, or uncertain policy application.

Technical Support is used only when the demonstration application or a synthetic integration fails.

2. Priority rules

Normal priority applies to a standard missing-document follow-up with no risk indicator. Its target due date is two business days after approval.

High priority applies when a request is blocked close to a synthetic service deadline or when multiple documents are invalid. Its target due date is one business day after approval.

Critical priority is reserved for suspected fraud or urgent compliance conditions. Critical cases must be escalated to a human specialist and must not be automatically resolved.

3. Required ticket fields

Every ticket must contain:

- A generated ticket ID.
- The related request ID.
- Ticket category and priority.
- A concise reason.
- The missing or exceptional items, when applicable.
- Supporting policy citations.
- Creation timestamp and current status.
- The identity of the approving demonstration user or an approval reference.

4. Human approval

The agent may draft a ticket, but it must show the proposed fields and wait for explicit approval before writing the ticket. A rejection must be recorded without creating the ticket.

5. Data minimization

Ticket descriptions should use request IDs rather than unnecessary applicant details. Sensitive document contents must not be copied into tickets.
