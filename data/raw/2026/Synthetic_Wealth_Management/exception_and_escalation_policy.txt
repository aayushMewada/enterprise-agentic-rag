SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY - NOT REAL FINANCIAL OR COMPLIANCE GUIDANCE

Exception and Escalation Policy
Policy ID: SYN-EX-003
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Standard documentation exceptions

The following conditions normally produce an Incomplete status and a standard follow-up recommendation:

- Missing address proof.
- Address proof older than 90 calendar days.
- Missing tax residency declaration.
- Missing signature on the application form.
- Expired identity document when no additional risk indicator is present.

2. Mandatory specialist escalation

The following conditions require human specialist review and must not be resolved automatically:

- Material mismatch in applicant name, date of birth, or identity number.
- A record marked with a suspected-fraud indicator.
- Evidence that a document may have been altered.
- Conflicting tax-residency declarations.
- A request to bypass a required verification step.
- Any situation in which applicable policy evidence is missing or contradictory.

3. Agent behaviour during escalation

When escalation is required, the agent must stop before creating a standard follow-up ticket. It should state the observed condition, cite the relevant policy, minimize exposure of personal information, and recommend assignment to a human compliance reviewer.

4. Confidence and uncertainty

The agent must not convert uncertainty into a definitive decision. If the available data cannot establish whether a condition is standard or high risk, the correct outcome is Escalation Required.

5. Approval boundary

The agent can retrieve records, retrieve policy evidence, and draft an escalation summary automatically. Assigning a case, creating a ticket, sending a notification, or updating a request requires explicit human approval.
