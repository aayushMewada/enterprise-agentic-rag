Document Verification Procedure
Procedure ID: SYN-DV-002
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

1. Purpose

This procedure describes the synthetic steps used to review documentation for an account-opening request.

2. Verification sequence

The reviewer should perform the following steps in order:

1. Retrieve the request by its request ID.
2. Confirm that the request type is Individual Account Opening.
3. Compare the received-document list with the required-document checklist.
4. Check expiry dates for identity documents.
5. Check that address proof was issued within the previous 90 calendar days.
6. Confirm that the application form and tax declaration are signed.
7. Compare the applicant name, date of birth, and identity number across documents.
8. Record missing, expired, stale, unsigned, or inconsistent items.
9. Apply the exception and escalation policy.
10. Prepare a recommendation for human review.

3. Outcome categories

Complete means every required document is present and no unresolved inconsistency has been detected.

Incomplete means one or more standard documents are missing, stale, expired, or unsigned, but no high-risk inconsistency has been identified.

Escalation Required means the record contains a material identity mismatch, a suspected fraud indicator, an unsupported document type, or another condition requiring specialist review.

4. Follow-up preparation

For an Incomplete request, a follow-up ticket should contain the request ID, missing-document list, relevant policy citation, proposed priority, and due date. The ticket must not contain unnecessary personal data.

5. Human responsibility

The agent may calculate completeness from synthetic records and propose the next step. A human remains responsible for approving write actions and making final decisions.
